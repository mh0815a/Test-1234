FICTIONAL CASE 2
Benton v. City Services
Court of Appeals of the Fictional State of Columbia
42 Col. App. 88, 501 P.3d 330 (2022)

NOTICE: This is an invented case and citation. It has no real-world legal authority.

FACTS:
Benton, age 56, was denied a promotion. The employer selected another candidate who had more recent experience managing projects similar to those involved in the open position.

RULE:
An employer may offer documented, job-related experience as a legitimate, nondiscriminatory reason for a promotion decision. The explanation must be honestly held and consistently applied. An employee may show pretext by presenting evidence that the stated reason is false, inconsistently applied, or not the actual reason for the decision.

HOLDING:
The court affirmed judgment for the employer because contemporaneous records supported the experience-based explanation and the employee did not produce sufficient evidence of pretext.
