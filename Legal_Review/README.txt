LEGAL REVIEW TEST PROJECT

All memo facts, parties, courts, statutes, case names, and citations in this project are fictional and created solely to test a legal memo review program. Do not cite or rely on them as real legal authority.

Folder setup:
- memo/legal_memo.txt: test memorandum
- cases/case_1.txt through case_3.txt: fictional opinions
- results/: place for the program's generated answer key
- legal_review.py: place your Python program here

Intended test:
The memo misstates Rivera v. Summit Group. The actual fictional opinion says a stray age-related remark alone does NOT automatically establish liability. The memo says it does and concludes the employer is liable on that basis. A working review agent should flag that misstatement and overstatement of the holding.
