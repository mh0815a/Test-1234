FICTIONAL CASE 1
Harper v. Northstar Analytics
Supreme Court of the Fictional State of Columbia
18 Col. 210, 455 P.3d 100 (2021)

NOTICE: This is an invented case and citation. It has no real-world legal authority.

FACTS:
Harper, age 61, applied for a department manager position. She met the minimum qualifications but was passed over for a substantially younger candidate.

RULE:
To establish an initial age-discrimination case under the fictional Age Equality in Employment Act, an employee must show that:
(1) she belongs to the protected age group;
(2) she was qualified for the position;
(3) she was denied the position; and
(4) the position went to a substantially younger person.

HOLDING:
The court held that Harper satisfied the initial elements. The court did not hold that satisfying these elements automatically proves discrimination. The employer may still offer a legitimate, nondiscriminatory explanation, and the employee may attempt to show that the explanation is pretextual.
