### Overall Assessment

- **2 of 3 citations (≈ 67 %) are accurate.**  
- **1 citation (≈ 33 %) is inaccurate.**  
- The inaccurate citation (Rivera) materially misstates the court’s holding and could mislead the supervisor in assessing the claim’s strength.

**Recommendations**

1. **Correct the Rivera passage** to accurately portray that an isolated age‑related remark is *insufficient* to prove discrimination without additional evidence of intent or pattern.  
2. **Retain the strong third‑party factual support** in the memo’s discussion (Harper and Benton) – these provide a solid factual and doctrinal basis for the claim.  
3. **E‑mail the supervising attorney** with the revised memo, highlighting the change to Rivera and noting the overall high level of accuracy.  

By making these adjustments, the memorandum will present a faithful, reliable legal analysis while ensuring that no unstated or mischaracterized authority is cited.
